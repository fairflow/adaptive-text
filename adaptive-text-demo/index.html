Adaptive Text Condensation

Text that adapts to your time & space

The Concept

Imagine text that automatically adjusts its level of detail based on how much space you give it. Expand the window or increase the detail level to see more comprehensive information. Shrink it down to get just the essential points. This demonstrates how AI-powered text condensation could revolutionize information consumption, allowing readers to match content density to their available time and interest level.
